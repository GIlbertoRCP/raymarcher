Physics Guide

Gravitational Lensing: The strong gravitational field of the black hole warps spacetime, forcing photons to follow curved paths. Light from behind the black hole is focused toward the observer, producing visual double images, distorted arches, and circular Einstein Rings.

Accretion Disk: Gas and dust orbiting the black hole form a thin, spinning disk. Because light is lensed, you can see both the top and bottom of the rear side of the disk, warped into halos that wrap around the event horizon.

Doppler Beaming: The side of the accretion disk rotating toward you appears significantly brighter and hotter (blueshifted) due to relativistic velocity. The side rotating away is dimmer and redder (redshifted).

Frame Dragging: A rotating (Kerr) black hole drags the very fabric of spacetime around with it. This breaks the spherical symmetry of the lensing and accretion disk, shifting the photon sphere and horizon shape relative to the spin axis.

Physics & Simulator Guide

This simulator renders a Schwarzschild / Kerr Black Hole in real-time by tracing individual photon trajectories (null geodesics) through a gravitationally curved spacetime.

1. General Relativity & Bending of Light

Light rays travel along curved paths around the mass. The integration uses a 4th-Order Runge-Kutta (RK4) solver to step the photon through the geodesic equation.

Radial Gravitational Force: a_grav = -M * (1 + u)⁵ * (2 - u) / (r³ * (1 - u)³) * x

Where M is mass, r is radius, and u = M / (2 * r) in isotropic coordinates.

2. Black Hole Spin (Frame Dragging)

A rotating (Kerr) black hole drags spacetime itself in the direction of spin. Spacetime rotation applies a gravitational Coriolis force that deflects light asymmetrically.

Frame Dragging: ω = 2 * J / (r³ * (1 + u)⁶), F_dragging = 2 * (v x ω)

Where J = M * spin is the angular momentum, v is velocity, and ω is frame dragging vector.

3. Accretion Disk Physics (True 3D Volumetric)

Superheated plasma orbits the event horizon at relativistic speeds. Rather than using a simplified flat plane intersection, the simulator integrates emission and absorption step-by-step through the 3D volume of the disk. Vertical noise shear is applied to construct volumetric swirls.

Thickness & Density: ρ(r, z) = ρ_density * (r_in / r_xy)²·⁵ * exp(-z² / (2 * disk_thickness²))

4. Doppler Beaming & Gravitational Redshift

The disk is highly asymmetrical due to two factors: (1) Doppler Beaming makes gas traveling towards you appear blindingly bright (blueshifted) and gas traveling away look dim (redshifted). (2) Gravitational Redshift drains light energy as it climbs out of the gravity well.

Total Shift: g_total = g_doppler * g_grav, T_observed = T_disk * g_total

5. Blackbody Radiation Fit

Colors are calculated by evaluating the observed temperature at each point in the disk and mapping the Planckian emission spectrum to standard sRGB space.

Planck Law: B_ν(T) = (2*h*ν³ / c²) * [ 1 / (e^(h*ν / k_B*T) - 1) ]
